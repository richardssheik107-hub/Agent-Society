# Research B1 — Rule calibration boundary

Research B1 produces descriptive candidate distributions only. It does not change `ActionType`, `RuleEngine`, the 06:00–24:00 simulator, or production configuration.

| Layer | Ownership | Examples | Research B1 decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — hard world rules | Deterministic state transitions and invariants | Actor and location exist; `money >= price`; stock > 0; actor is at seller; no conflicting simultaneous activities; state remains valid | **KEEP DETERMINISTIC.** Human frequency data cannot relax validity. |
| B — empirical parameters | Future calibrated priors, not commands | Episode duration, activity start distributions, daily WORK/LEISURE/SLEEP minutes, transition priors, mobility frequency, contact frequency | **CANDIDATE FOR CALIBRATION.** Compare median and IQR; require a separately approved integration study before production change. |
| C — agent choice | Context-sensitive model decision | What to do now, whether to follow or deviate from a common pattern, how needs and obligations affect a choice | **KEEP MODEL-DRIVEN.** Empirical priors inform context but never force an action at a clock time. |

No `12:00 → EAT`, `09:00 → WORK`, or `23:00 → SLEEP` script follows from a diary histogram. `HumanActivityType.MOVE` is empirical travel/time-use, not automatically an executable simulator location transition. `OTHER` is a real aggregate of unmapped activities, not the simulator idle failure metric.

Current uncalibrated simulator baseline is in `src/social_sim/daily/time.py`: WORK episode 90 min, LEISURE episode 60 min, SLEEP episode 360 min. `EAT` in `src/social_sim/rules/eating.py` is an immediate consumption effect, not a timed episode, so ATUS EAT duration is **NOT_COMPARABLE** without a simulator timing definition. The simulator runs 06:00–24:00, while ATUS diaries cover a 04:00–04:00 24-hour period, so simulator day-total SLEEP/WORK/LEISURE cannot be directly equated to ATUS 24-hour daily totals. Once validated microdata are available, episode durations can be compared to ATUS employed-adult weekday medians and IQRs: below p25 = `TOO_LOW`, above p75 = `TOO_HIGH`, otherwise `WITHIN_EMPIRICAL_RANGE`. Until then, all empirical statuses are `NOT_COMPARABLE` due to missing measured values.

Research B1.1 adds a separate NHAPS/AHTUS 1992–94 adult pilot. Its actual `main`/`eloc`/`mtrav`/`recwght` SPSS metadata support descriptive episode durations, 24-hour daily allocation, midnight-clock start bins, transitions, and activity × source-location associations. For the NHAPS comparison only, session values below the employed-weekday episode p25 are `BELOW_EMPIRICAL_IQR`, within p25–p75 `WITHIN_EMPIRICAL_IQR`, and above p75 `ABOVE_EMPIRICAL_IQR`; absent timed semantics are `NOT_COMPARABLE`. This does not supersede the ATUS adapter. NHAPS is 1990s U.S. time use, not a 2026 universal lifestyle. Hard money, inventory, location, seller, and state invariants remain deterministic; survey data never create a forced daily schedule or override agent choices. Empirical MOVE is time-use travel, not the simulator's executable MOVE. No production parameters or actions are changed.
