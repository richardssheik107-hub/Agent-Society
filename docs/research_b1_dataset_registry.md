ATUS was the initial B1 priority because its official single-year activity and respondent files are small, its [2025 dictionary](https://www.bls.gov/tus/dictionaries/atusintcodebk25.pdf) identifies episode-level fields, and its [2025 lexicon](https://www.bls.gov/tus/lexicons/lexiconnoex2025.pdf) supports auditable mapping. Automated access returned HTTP 403 locally and in CI, so the official CTUR NHAPS adult survey became the B1.1 pilot; the [B1.1 report](research_b1_1_nhaps_ahtus_calibration.md) records the actual schema and statistics. The ATUS roster remains necessary for its future adapter: the 2025 dictionary places `TEAGE` on Roster, not Activity/Respondent. Who-present data are not a continuous contact network.

Neither mobility traces nor contact networks are interchangeable with time-use diaries. GeoLife has sensitive precise location histories and no representative daily activity sample. SocioPatterns represents specific settings and their distinct licensing regimes. MTUS and other AHTUS surveys remain future validation sources; the NHAPS package is a distinct official CTUR source, not an ATUS mirror.
